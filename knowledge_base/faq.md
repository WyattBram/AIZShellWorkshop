# ShellHacks FAQ

## When is ShellHacks?
ShellHacks 2026 runs for 24 hours, starting Saturday 9:00 AM and ending Sunday 9:00 AM.

## Where is it held?
FIU's Ryder Business Building, Miami, FL.

## Who can participate?
Any currently enrolled student, undergrad or grad, from any school. Teams are 1-4 people.

## How is judging done?
Projects are judged on: Innovation, Technical Complexity, Design, and Completion. Each team gets a 3-minute demo + 2-minute Q&A with judges.

## What should I submit?
A working demo, a public GitHub repo, and a Devpost submission with a short writeup and video by the deadline.

## Is there a code of conduct?
Yes. Harassment, plagiarism, and pre-built projects (started before the hackathon) are not allowed and will be disqualified.

## What if my team has a question during the event?
Flag down any volunteer wearing a orange lanyard, or post in the #help channel on the event Discord.
